Marketing Kya Hai?
Intro
01 Marketing — Simple Definition ▶
American Marketing Association (AMA) ka definition

Marketing woh process hai jisme tum kuch valuable banate ho, communicate karte ho, deliver karte ho, aur exchange karte ho — customers ke liye, society ke liye.

🔑 Simple Bhaasha mein: Marketing matlab hai — sahi product, sahi insaan ko, sahi jagah, sahi time pe, sahi price pe dena — aur unhe batana ki kyun khareedein.

Marketing sirf advertisement nahi hai! Yeh ek poora system hai — research se lekar product banana, price set karna, distribution aur promotion sab kuch.

Component 01
Marketing Research
Market ko samajhna — customer kya chahta hai, competition kya kar raha hai.
Component 02
Product Development
Research ke baad ek aisa product banana jo customer ki zaroorat poori kare.
Component 03
Pricing Strategy
Product ki sahi price decide karna — na bahut zyada, na bahut kam.
Component 04
Advertising & Promotion
Logon ko batana ki product exist karta hai aur kyun khareedein.
Component 05
Sales & Distribution
Product ko customer tak pahunchana — store ho, online ho ya seedha ghar pe.
Component 06
CRM
Customer Relationship Management — existing customers ko happy rakhna aur retain karna.
02 Marketing ka Interdisciplinary Nature ▶

Marketing sirf business subject nahi hai — yeh Psychology, Sociology, aur Economics teeno ka combination hai.

Psychology

Consumer ke dimaag ko samajhna — woh kyu kuch khareedta hai, emotions kya role play karti hain, kaise usse convince karein.

💡Example: Amul ka tagline "Utterly Butterly Delicious" — nostalgia aur positive emotion trigger karta hai.
Sociology

Society aur culture ka product pe kya impact padta hai — family, community, trends.

💡Example: Tata Nano fail hua kyunki India mein car status symbol hai — "cheap car" bolne se log door bhage.
Economics

Pricing, demand-supply, value proposition — kya price pe kya value milti hai yeh important hai.

💡Example: Amul affordable price pe quality dairy product deta hai — economies of scale se rural tak bhi pahuncha.
Real Lesson

Tata Nano ek affordable car tha — economically sahi, psychologically galat! "Duniya ka sabse sasta car" positioning ne aspirational buyers ko bhaga diya.

⚠️Lesson: Price aur value sahi ho, but agar psychology aur sociology wrong ho — product fail hoga!
03 Entrepreneurial Marketing vs Regular Marketing ▶
📌Regular Marketing: Badi company karta hai — resources hain, team hai, budget hai.
🚀Entrepreneurial Marketing: Limited resources mein innovative, proactive, aur risk-taking approach se marketing karna. Unique value deliver karna focus hai.

Startup founder ke liye yahi important hai — jugaad marketing! Zero budget mein maximum impact.

Marketing Ka History
Timeline
01 Marketing ka Evolution — Barter se Digital Tak ▶
Barter Era
Pehle Zamaane — Cheez ke Badle Cheez
Paise nahi the, to log ek dusre ko goods exchange karte the. Kisan apna anaj deta tha, badle mein kapda milta tha. Yahi original "marketing" tha!
1800s
Production Era — "Banao, Bik Jayega"
Industrial revolution ke time manufacturers ka attitude tha — "If we make it, they will buy it." Manufacturing efficiency pe focus tha. Customer ki koi nahi sunta tha.
1920-40s
Sales Era — "Becho Becho Becho!"
Factories ne itna produce kiya ki surplus ho gaya. Ab aggressive sales tactics shuru hue — door-to-door selling, pushy salesmen. Customer ki zaroorat secondary tha, sales primary.
1950-80s
Marketing Era — Customer is King!
Pehli baar customer ki zaroorat central bani. 4 Ps formally introduce hue (1960, E. Jerome McCarthy). Pehle research karo ki customer kya chahta hai, phir banao.
1990s+
Digital Era — Personalized Marketing
Internet aaya, social media aaya, data-driven marketing shuru hua. Amazon, Google, Facebook — sab customer data use kar ke personalized ads dikhate hain.
Now
Sustainability Era — Green Marketing
Ab sirf profit nahi, environment aur society bhi important hai. Brands jo genuinely sustainable hain — aur jo sirf "greenwash" karte hain — dono exist karte hain.
🤔Exam Question: 1960 ka framework (4Ps) aaj 2026 mein bhi kyun relevant hai? Kyunki fundamentals change nahi hote — sirf tools change hote hain!

Core Concepts — Need, Want, Demand, Value
Basics
01 Need vs Want vs Demand — Difference Kya Hai? ▶
Concept 01
Need (Zaroorat)
Jo bilkul basic hai — bina jis ke jeena mushkil ho. Bhookh lagna, pyaas lagna, kapde chahiye, shelter chahiye. Marketing create nahi karta, yeh pehle se exist karta hai.
Concept 02
Want (Chahna)
Need ko specific form dena. Bhookh hai (need) → pizza chahiye (want). Pyaas hai (need) → Coca Cola chahiye (want). Culture aur society wants shape karte hain.
Concept 03
Demand
Want + Buying Power = Demand. Bahut logon ko Lamborghini chahiye (want) but afford nahi kar sakte, isliye demand limited hai. Demand = Want + Paisa.
Concept 04
Value
Customer ko lagta hai ki jo wo pay kar raha hai, usse milne wali benefit uske worth hai. Value = Benefits ÷ Cost. Agar benefits zyada aur cost kam — high value!
Concept 05
Satisfaction
Product ne jo expect kiya tha, wo mila ya nahi? Expected performance vs Actual performance. Expectation se zyada mila = Delight. Kam mila = Disappointment.
Concept 06
Exchange
Marketing ka core — kuch valuable dene ke badle mein kuch valuable lena. Paise ke badle product, ya service ke badle service. Dono parties satisfied honi chahiye.
🔑Trick to Remember: Pehle Need hoti hai → Marketing usse Want mein convert karta hai → Buying power se Demand banta hai → Sahi product se Value milti hai → Satisfaction ata hai.

Module 02 // Strategy
4Ps of Marketing — The Core Framework
Key Framework
📌History: 4Ps formally 1960 mein E. Jerome McCarthy ne diye, aur Neil Borden ke "Marketing Mix" concept se inspire tha (1950s). Aaj 2026 mein bhi yeh relevant hai kyunki fundamentals change nahi hote!
P1 Product — Kya Bechoge? ▶
Product = Jo tum market ko offer karte ho — goods, services, ya experience

Product sirf ek physical cheez nahi hai — yeh ek "bundle" hai: quality, design, packaging, brand name, warranty, after-sales service — sab milake product banta hai.

Product ke Core Questions
  • Mera product kya hai? Kya karta hai?
  • Customer ki kaunsi zaroorat poori karta hai?
  • Competitors se alag kya hai?
  • Kiska liye hai? (Target audience)
  • Koi unfilled need to nahi hai market mein?
Product Life Cycle (PLC)
  • Introduction — Naya product launch, slow growth, loss ho sakta hai
  • Growth — Sales tezi se badhti hai, competitors aate hain
  • Maturity — Sales peak pe, competition intense
  • Decline — Sales girne lagti, ya to reinvent karo ya chhod do
💡Taylor Swift Example: Uska "product" sirf music nahi hai — merch, concerts, films, courses, fashion — yeh ek complete product ecosystem hai! Aur woh har stage pe reinvent karti hai.

Services ke liye extra 3Ps bhi add hote hain: People (staff), Process (delivery), Physical Evidence (tangible proof). Isliye total 7Ps bante hain services mein.

📌Services ki 4 Khasiyatein (IPIV): Intangibility (chhu nahi sakte), Inseparability (service aur provider saath hote hain), Perishability (unsold seat expire ho jati hai), Variability (quality vary ho sakti hai).
P2 Price — Kitna Charge Karoge? ▶
Price = Jo customer pay karta hai + perceived value ka signal

Price setting tricky hai — bahut zyada lagao to customer nahi khareeday, bahut kam lagao to "cheap" lag sakta hai aur grow karna impossible ho jayega.

₹999 vs ₹1000 — fark lagta hai psychologically
StrategyMatlabIndia Example

PenetrationPehle low price rakho, market share loJio — Free data se shuru
SkimmingPehle high price, phir gradually kam karoiPhone launch pe high price

⚠️Tata Nano Lesson: Sasta price = low quality signal. "Sabse sasta car" positioning ne aspirational buyers ko repel kar diya. Price sirf number nahi, ek message hai!
P3 Place — Kahan Milega? ▶
Place = Product kaise aur kahan customer tak pahunche — distribution channels

Distribution ka matlab hai — product ko factory se customer ke haath tak pohonchane ka poora system.

Type 01
Intensive Distribution
Har jagah available — har dukaan, har store. Lifebuoy soap HUL — har kirana store mein. Mass market products ke liye.
Type 02
Selective Distribution
Sirf kuch selected outlets mein. Samsung premium phones sirf authorized stores pe.
Type 03
Exclusive Distribution
Ek hi outlet ya limited locations. Apple Store, Rolex showroom. Luxury feel ke liye.
Type 04
D2C — Direct to Consumer
Seedha customer tak — beech mein koi middleman nahi. Licious meat directly deliver karta hai. Full control + first-party data.
🔑Channel Conflict: Jab manufacturer khud online bechne lage to traditional distributors protest karte hain. Udaan aur JioMart ne traditional wholesalers ko bypass kiya — woh angry ho gaye!
P4 Promotion — Batao, Convince Karo! ▶
Promotion = Logon ko batana ki product exist karta hai aur kyun khareedein

Promotion = Advertising + PR + Sales Promotion + Personal Selling + Social Media — sab milake Integrated Marketing Communications (IMC) banta hai.

Advertising ka Role

Awareness banata hai, positioning establish karta hai, memory mein brand store karta hai. LIC ke ads isliye chalte rehte hain — jab zaroorat padegi, pehle LIC yaad aayega.

Advertising vs Promotion

Advertising = long-term brand building.
Promotion = short-term action trigger. "50% off — Aaj Hi!" wala kaam promotion karta hai.

💡Apple Example: Marketing ne premium positioning define ki → Advertising ne aspiration build ki → Promotions almost nahi ke barabar hain → Salesperson store experience se close karta hai. Sab aligned!

STP — Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning
Strategy
🔑Golden Rule: Agar STP galat hai, to 4Ps kabhi bhi product ko save nahi kar sakta. Pehle sahi log find karo, phir unke liye sahi marketing karo.
S Segmentation — Market Ko Todna ▶
Segmentation = Bade market ko chote groups mein todna jinka similar needs hain

Ek hi product sab ke liye nahi hota. Market ko useful groups mein divide karo.

Lifestyle, values, interests
TypeMatlabExample
GeographicLocation ke basis peNorth India mein chai, South mein filter coffee
DemographicAge, gender, income, educationTeenager vs Senior citizen — different products
PsychographicGym-goer ke liye protein supplements
BehavioralUsage patterns, loyaltyHeavy user vs occasional user — different offers
T Targeting — Sahi Group Chunna ▶
Targeting = Sabse attractive segment ko choose karna aur focus karna

Har segment pe focus karna possible nahi — resources limited hain. Isliye best segment chunna zaroori hai.

Achha Target Segment Kaisa Hota Hai?
  • Measurable — Size aur buying power measure ho sake
  • Substantial — Itna bada ho ki profitable ho
  • Accessible — Reach karna possible ho
  • Actionable — Effective programs design ho sakein
Targeting Strategies
  • Mass Marketing — Sab ke liye ek product (Maggi)
  • Niche Marketing — Bahut specific group (Organic baby food)
  • Segmented — Multiple segments, different offers
  • Micro/1:1 — Individual level (Netflix recommendations)
P Positioning — Dimag Mein Jagah Banana ▶
Positioning = Customer ke dimaag mein apna unique space banana competitors se alag

Positioning matlab hai — jab customer teri category sune, to tere brand ka naam pehle yaad aaye.

Volvo
Positioned As: Safety
Volvo = Safe car — yeh dimag mein ghus gaya hai. Har communication mein safety.
Maggi
Positioned As: 2-Minute Noodles
Speed aur convenience — "bas 2 minute" yeh line dimag mein baith gayi generations tak.
Amul
Positioned As: Taste of India
Desh ki dairy brand — affordable, trustworthy, familiar. Cultural positioning.
Jio
Positioned As: Affordable Internet
India ko digital karna — free/cheap data se mass internet adoption. Disruption strategy.
⚠️Wrong Positioning = Fail: Tata Nano positioned tha "world's cheapest car" — this attracted nobody. Aspirational buyers wanted value, not cheapness. Positioning ne unhe alienate kar diya.

Market Research — Data Se Decisions
Research
01 Market Research Kya Hai aur Kyun Zaruri? ▶

Market Research woh process hai jisme tum information gather, analyze aur interpret karte ho — consumer needs, market trends, aur business opportunities samajhne ke liye.

↓Risk Reduce Karta Hai
↑Better Targeting Enable Karta Hai
✓Market Gaps Identify Karta Hai
💡Pricing Decisions Mein Help Karta Hai
💡Example: Nestlé ne Maggi Oats Noodles launch karne se pehle consumer taste tests kiye — health-conscious Indians accept karenge ya nahi? Yahi smart research hai!
02 Types of Research — Primary vs Secondary ▶
Primary Research

Khud data collect karna — firsthand, fresh data. Zyada accurate but costly aur time-consuming.

  • Surveys — Questionnaire se data (Google Forms)
  • Focus Groups — Chhota group, deep discussion
  • Interviews — One-on-one conversation
  • Observation — Consumer ko dekhna actually
💡Zomato ne 10,000 users survey kiye Zomato Gold launch se pehle — food ya drinks pe discounts zyada pasand hain?
Secondary Research

Pehle se available data use karna — faster, cheaper but might be outdated ya irrelevant.

  • Government Reports — Census, FSSAI data
  • Industry Reports — McKinsey, Gartner
  • Google Trends — Search trends
  • Competitor Analysis — Unka data study karo
💡Swiggy ne FSSAI reports analyze kiye grocery delivery mein enter karne se pehle.
03 Qualitative vs Quantitative Research ▶
AspectQualitativeQuantitative
Kya dhundhte hain?Why — Motivations, feelings, beliefsHow many — Numbers, patterns, statistics
MethodsInterviews, Focus Groups, EthnographySurveys, Polls, Usage Data, Experiments
Sample SizeChhota — 10-20 logBada — 100 to thousands
OutputInsights, quotes, themesCharts, percentages, statistics
India ExampleTanishq ne jewellery ki emotional meaning explore kiUber real-time data se surge pricing adjust karta hai
🔑Best Practice: Pehle Qualitative research karo — "kya problem hai" samajhne ke liye. Phir Quantitative — "kitna bada problem hai" measure karne ke liye.
04 Survey Design + Sampling Basics ▶
Achha Survey Kaise Banate Hain?
  • Simple questions rakhna — jargon avoid karo
  • Leading questions avoid karo ("Don't you agree..." type mat pucho)
  • Neutral raho — apna bias mat daalo
  • Likert scale use karo (1-5 rating)
  • Logical sequence rakhna — easy se tough
Sampling Types
  • Random — Lottery jaisa — har kisi ka equal chance
  • Stratified — Groups banao, phir random — age wise, gender wise
  • Convenience — Jo available hai usse survey karo — fast but biased
  • Cluster — Geography ke hisab se groups

02 Purchase ko Influence Karne Wale Factors ▶
Factor 01
Cultural Factors
Culture, subculture, social class — India mein family ka bahut bada role hai purchase mein. Diwali mein gold khareedna cultural tradition hai.
Factor 02
Social Factors
Reference groups (friends, family), opinion leaders (influencers), social status. "Doston mein sab iPhone use karte hain" = peer pressure purchase.
Factor 03
Personal Factors
Age, occupation, income, lifestyle, personality. 20 saal ka student aur 40 saal ka professional — same product nahi khareedenge.
Factor 04
Psychological Factors
Motivation, perception, learning, beliefs, attitudes. "Only 5 left!" = Scarcity creates urgency. FOMO marketing yahi use karta hai!
💡Behavioral Economics Trick: "Only 5 left!" = artificial scarcity → urgency badh jaati hai → impulse purchase hota hai. Amazon, Zomato sab use karte hain yeh tactic.

Module 03 // Execution
Sales Management & Distribution
Execution
01 Sales vs Marketing — Kya Fark Hai? ▶
Marketing

"Pull" strategy — awareness banao, interest create karo, demand generate karo. Customer khud aaye tumhare paas.

Long-term focus — brand building, positioning, relationships.

Sales

"Push" strategy — interested prospect ko final purchase tak le jaana. Deal close karna.

Short-term focus — quotas, revenue targets, closing deals.

🔑Best Performance ke liye: Marketing aur Sales ko aligned hona chahiye — dono ko same target customer, same message. Jab yeh misaligned hote hain — leads waste hote hain!
Marketing MetricsSales Metrics
Brand Awareness, ImpressionsRevenue, Deals Closed
Website Traffic, Leads GeneratedConversion Rate, Average Deal Size
Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)Quota Attainment %
NPS (Net Promoter Score)Sales Cycle Length
02 Sales Ke Types — Inside vs Field ▶
Type 01
Inside Sales
Office ya ghar se remotely sell karna — phone, email, Zoom calls. Bangalore SaaS startup globally bechta hai bina client ke paas gaye. Cost effective!
Type 02
Field Sales
Physical milna — client ke office ya store jaana. High-value B2B deals mein important. Relationship building zyada hoti hai.
Type 03
B2B Sales
Business to Business — ek company doosri company ko bechti hai. Longer sales cycle, bigger deals, multiple decision makers.
Type 04
B2C Sales
Business to Consumer — direct end customer ko bechna. Faster decisions, emotional buying, mass marketing kaam aata hai.
03 Distribution Channels — Product Customer Tak Kaise Pahunche ▶

Distribution channel = Product factory se customer ke haath tak pohonchane ka rasta.

  • Zero Level (D2C) — Manufacturer → Customer (Licious, Tesla). Maximum control, first-party data, but expensive infrastructure.
  • One Level — Manufacturer → Retailer → Customer (Amul → Big Bazaar → Tum). Common in FMCG.
  • Two Level — Manufacturer → Wholesaler → Retailer → Customer. Traditional India distribution model — HUL ka poora network.
  • Online/Marketplace — Amazon, Flipkart via platform. Wide reach but platform takes commission aur data.
⚠️Channel Conflict Example: Jab JioMart aur Udaan ne directly retailers ko sell kiya, traditional wholesalers protest karne lage — unka business bypass ho raha tha. Yahi "vertical channel conflict" hai.
💡Strategic Alliance Example: Toyota aur Suzuki India mein platform share karte hain — Grand Vitara aur Hyryder same platform pe hain. Collaboration instead of competition!
04 Ethics in Sales — Galat Kya Hai? ▶

Sales mein pressure bahut hota hai quota meet karne ka — yahi kabhi kabhi unethical behavior lead karta hai.

⚠️Real Example: Insurance agent ne customer ko aisi policy bech di jo uske liye suitable nahi thi — sirf apna monthly quota meet karne ke liye. Yeh Ethics in Selling ka violation hai.
  • Misleading information dena — galat claims
  • High pressure tactics — customer ko force karna
  • Unsuitable products push karna (mis-selling)
  • Hidden charges nahi batana
🔑Long-Term Lesson: Short-term mein quota meet ho sakta hai unethical se, but long-term mein reputation aur trust khatam ho jaata hai. Ethical selling = sustainable business.

Advertising = Purchase se pehle dimag mein brand bithana

03 Message Strategy — Kya Bolo aur Kaise Bolo? ▶
Rational Message Strategy

Logic aur facts se convince karo. Features, benefits, performance data dikhao.

📌Car ads mein mileage figures, washing powder mein whiteness comparison — yahi rational appeal hai.
Emotional Message Strategy

Feelings se connect karo — love, fear, humor, nostalgia, pride.

📌Cadbury's ads — relationships aur celebrations ko show karte hain. Tata Tea "Jaago Re" — social awakening emotion use karta hai.
Appeal 01
Fear Appeal
"Yeh nahi kiya to kya hoga" — Insurance ads, helmet ads. Fear motivate karta hai action ke liye.
Appeal 02
Humor Appeal
Hasao, yaad raho! Fevicol ke ads — memorable humor. Risk = agar humor appropriate nahi to brand suffer kare.
Appeal 03
Aspirational Appeal
Woh life dikha jo customer banana chahta hai. Luxury brands — Rolex, BMW — dream sell karte hain, product nahi.

05 IMC — Integrated Marketing Communications ▶
IMC = Sab promotional tools ko integrate karna ek consistent message ke liye

Alag alag channels pe alag alag message mat do — ek unified brand voice rakhna zaroori hai.

  • Advertising — TV, digital, print, outdoor (paid)
  • Public Relations (PR) — News coverage, press releases, events (earned)
  • Sales Promotion — Discounts, offers, coupons, contests (time-bound)
  • Personal Selling — Direct human interaction
  • Digital Marketing — Social media, SEO, email marketing
  • Influencer Marketing — Creators aur celebrities through social media
💡Apple IMC Example: Store experience + TV ads + Social media + PR launches sab ek consistent "premium, simple, powerful" message dete hain. Koi confusion nahi!

01 Loyalty Program Kya Hota Hai? ▶
Loyalty Program = Repeat customers ko reward karna so they keep coming back

A loyalty program ek marketing strategy hai jo customers ko bar bar aane ke liye rewards, discounts, ya incentives deti hai.

True loyalty sirf rewards se nahi aati — yeh aati hai Brand Trust, Habit, Switching Cost, aur Emotional Connection se.

🔑Loyalty Program ke Goals: Customer churn reduce karna + Repeat purchases encourage karna + Brand advocates banana (jo doosron ko recommend karein).
02 6 Types of Loyalty Programs — India Examples ▶
Type 01
Points-Based
Har purchase pe points milte hain, baad mein redeem karo. Shoppers Stop First Citizen Card — ₹100 spend = X points, accumulate karo aur discount lo.
Type 02
Tiered Membership
Zyada spend = zyada benefits. Levels hote hain — Silver, Gold, Platinum. Taj InnerCircle — jitna stay karo, utne better perks. Airlines mein bhi yahi model.
Type 03
Paid Subscription
Monthly/annual fee pay karo, exclusive benefits lo. Amazon Prime, Swiggy One — pay karo, unlimited free delivery + exclusive deals.
Type 04
Cashback
Purchase pe directly paise wapas milte hain. Simple aur immediate reward. HDFC Cashback Credit Card — har transaction pe % cashback.
Type 05
Coalition Program
Multiple brands milke ek loyalty program. Reliance HappyPoints — Reliance Fresh, Digital, Trends — sab jagah points earn karo, kahi bhi redeem karo.
Type 06
Gamified Loyalty
Game jaisi engagement — challenges, quizzes, milestones. Zomato Gold — dining privileges + gamified predictions aur quizzes. Fun + loyalty = powerful combo.
03 Successful Loyalty Program Kaise Banate Hain? ▶
  • Step 1 — Objective Define Karo: Repeat purchase chahiye ya referrals ya engagement? Clear goal set karo pehle.
  • Step 2 — Type Choose Karo: Apne business ke hisab se — FMCG ke liye points, B2C subscription ke liye paid model better hai.
  • Step 3 — Rewards Design Karo: Earning rules clear rakho (₹1 = 1 point?). Redemption easy honi chahiye. Perceived value high hona chahiye!
  • Step 4 — Technology: CRM system, app, data tracking — sab integrate karo. Data se insights lo.
  • Step 5 — Measure Karo: Churn rate, repeat purchase frequency, CLV (Customer Lifetime Value) track karo.
⚠️Common Mistake: Rewards ka perceived value low ho — customer ko lagta hai earn karna mushkil hai aur milta kuch nahi. Program flop! Example: Airline miles jo expire ho jaate hain — customers frustrated ho jaate hain.
💡Netflix Model (Tiered Subscription): Different tiers — Basic, Standard, Premium — alag screens, alag quality. Segmented loyalty jo different usage patterns ko address karta hai.
04 CRM — Customer Relationship Management ▶
CRM = Technology + Strategy to manage all customer interactions aur data

CRM sirf ek software nahi hai — yeh ek philosophy hai jo customer ko business ke center mein rakhti hai.

CRM Kya Karta Hai?
  • Customer data store karna — purchases, preferences, complaints
  • Personalized communication enable karna
  • High-value customers identify karna (VIP treatment)
  • Churn predict karna — kaun jaane wala hai?
India Examples
  • Zomato — exactly jaanta hai tum 11 PM pe kya order karte ho
  • Amazon — "Customers who bought this also bought..."
  • Netflix — personalized recommendations from watching history
  • Jio — data use patterns se personalized offers
🔑CLV — Customer Lifetime Value: Ek customer apni poori zindagi mein kitna paisa tera business ko dega. Isko maximize karna = loyalty programs ka asli goal. Ek loyal customer 10 new customers se zyada valuable ho sakta hai!
